# GU-AIOS Enterprise Architecture
Version: 1.0

Author:
Prof. Mohanad Alfarras

Project:
Gulf University Artificial Intelligence Operating System (GU-AIOS)

---

# Vision

To establish Gulf University as a leading AI-driven university through an integrated Artificial Intelligence Operating System that enhances decision making, institutional knowledge management, academic quality, operational efficiency, and digital transformation.

---

# Mission

Develop an enterprise AI ecosystem capable of supporting every department through specialized AI Agents, standardized workflows, centralized knowledge, and secure integrations.

---

# Objectives

1. Centralize institutional knowledge.
2. Improve decision making.
3. Automate repetitive processes.
4. Support academic quality.
5. Increase operational efficiency.
6. Ensure AI governance.
7. Protect institutional knowledge.
8. Build scalable AI infrastructure.

---

# Core Principles

- Human-Centered AI
- Security by Design
- Explainable AI
- Trusted Knowledge
- Modular Architecture
- Model Agnostic
- Privacy First
- Continuous Improvement

---

# Architecture Layers

6. Integration Layer
7. Governance Layer
8. Infrastructure Layer
